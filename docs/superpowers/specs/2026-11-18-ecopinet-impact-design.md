# EcoPinet · 02 Impact redesign

## Why
Bua felt the Impact section was "just saying stuff": every card shouted (emoji tags, big numbers, meter, dark block, colour blocks), and nothing backed the claims up. She wants it less blocky, with emphasis, but not too much.

## Goals
- Proof over claims: real photos and logos carry the emphasis.
- One clear hero (AIHM 1st place), quieter supporting rows.
- Calm: no emoji tags, no meter, no colour-block cards, no dark numbers block.

## Non-goals
- Changing 03 · Validation (it keeps its own photos: win-aihm with presenter, wide Agrithon stage).
- Changing copy beyond trimming what the new layout drops.

## Approaches
1. **Photo-led rows (picked):** hero photo + headline for AIHM, smaller thumbnail rows for Diamond Challenge (logo) and Agrithon (photo), numbers as one quiet line. Proof without repeating Validation's photos, since Bua supplied different shots.
2. Keep the cards, tone down colours: still blocky, still claims without proof.
3. Merge Impact into Validation: bigger restructure, loses the at-a-glance summary.

## Design
- Hero: `impact-aihm.jpg` (team pointing at the check) left, about 60% width; right: "1st place" large, "AIHM Business Plan Competition 2025", one line: "Out of 35 teams across Asia, judged by hospitality executives."
- Rows below, separated by thin rules: thumbnail + bold line + one sentence.
  - `logo-diamond.png`: "Top 20 · Diamond Challenge 2025": "Social innovation track. 400+ teams from 56 countries."
  - `impact-agrithon.jpg` (trio on set): "On national TV · Agrithon": "The first youth-led team on the show, broadcast on GMM25 and Channel 3."
- Numbers: one line, four items with thin dividers, number in deep green, label muted. Keep the count-up.
- Background: plain page (drop the green panel) or a very soft tint; builder's call, keep it quiet.
- Hover: gentle lift on photos only.
- Phone (390): stack, photo first; numbers 2×2.
